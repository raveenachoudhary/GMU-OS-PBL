COMMAND-LINE DIRECTORY LISTING
------------------------------
DIR lists root-directory filenames in four columns and prints the number
of matching files. Use "DIR BIN" or "DIR TXT" to list only files with
that extension. Other arguments are rejected with a usage message. LS
retains the detailed directory listing.

DIR reads the root FAT12 directory through os_get_file_list in
source/features/disk.asm. That routine omits deleted entries, directories,
volume labels and long-filename markers; DIR applies the optional extension
filter and counts the names returned by the OS routine.

Controlled floppy test (root entries: KERNEL.BIN, DEMO.BIN, NOTES.TXT,
OTHER.BAS):
  DIR:     shows all four names; Total matching files: 4
  DIR BIN: shows KERNEL.BIN and DEMO.BIN; Total matching files: 2
  DIR TXT: shows NOTES.TXT; Total matching files: 1
Boot disk_images/gmuos-dir-test.flp in QEMU, choose Cancel for the CLI,
then enter DIR, DIR BIN, and DIR TXT to reproduce these results.

The total counts the entries returned by os_get_file_list, not directories
or FAT volume labels.
